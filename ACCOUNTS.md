# 🔑 Curvada's Kitchen Portal Accounts

This document contains the simulation portal credentials and access permissions for the **Workplace Console**.

---

Select your role on the login screen and enter the corresponding passcode to sign in:

| Role | Passcode | Accessible Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🍳 Kitchen Staff** | `kitchen123` | Active Order Queue (dispatching, cooking status updates) |
| **👑 Administrator** | `admin123` | All panels (Orders, Inventory Stock, Menu Builder, Financials) |

---

## 🛠️ Access Level Breakdown

### 👨‍🍳 Kitchen Staff
- **Order Queue**: View pending orders, mark orders as "Preparing", "Dispatched", or "Delivered".
- *Restrictions*: Cannot modify menu prices, edit recipe requirements, manage stock/purchases, or view financial profit/loss records.

### 💼 Manager / Administrator
- **Order Queue**: Full queue management.
- **Stock & Inventory**:
  - Add raw material stock ingredients (with bulk converters).
  - Low-stock automatic alert thresholds.
  - Prep level forecast targets.
  - Export custom restock shopping lists.
- **Culinary Menu Builder**: Add/edit/delete dishes, configure recipe ingredient requirements, and toggle availability.
- **Financial Tracker**: Review total sales revenue, active cooking costs, delivered order transaction records, and reset/clear database actions.
